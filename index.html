Why Rockwood

It started with a favor for a friend. Aaron's wife wouldn't let him bring rocks into the house to display his rock crawlers — so we set out to build something she'd actually want on the shelf.

The result was the first Rockwood Display Obstacle: a one-of-a-kind, handmade display built for mini and micro RC crawlers. Most people will spend two hundred to seven hundred dollars on a rig, then set it on a five-dollar stand that was never built to last. That never made sense to us.

A prized rig deserves a display built with the same care — something you can appreciate as a piece of art, and still drive your rig across for fun. Leave it there for show, or take it for a climb whenever the mood strikes.

01

Hand-chosen stone

Every display starts with a single piece of visually striking natural stone, usually granite, selected by hand.

02

Hand-built scaffold

A wood scaffold is built by hand around the stone — its design echoes old mineshaft timber while following the rock's natural contours.

03

Integrated lighting

Built-in lighting brings each display to life in low-light settings, on a shelf or in a case.

04

Only natural materials

Just rock and wood — no plastic, no shortcuts. A non-scratch wooden base keeps it safe on any surface.

Modular by Design

Build a course

Every Rockwood display stands on its own — but it doesn't have to stay that way. Using a link set, individual displays connect to one another, so your collection can grow into a full obstacle course over time.
